Helplines & support

If you're having a hard time, please don't wait until it gets worse. These services are made for young people. Most of them are free, anonymous, and open 24/7. You don't need to know what to say — they'll help you find the words.

Life-threatening emergency? Call 112 immediately (Austria & EU-wide emergency number).

At school

  • Speak to your homeroom teacher or to a counsellor — they can listen to you and help you.
  • Talk with teachers or staff members you trust.
  • At VIS, counsellors and the student support team are available to support students and help them feel safe.
  • If school stress, bullying, or personal problems are affecting you, do not be afraid to ask for help.
  • Remember that you are not alone, and there are people at school who care about your wellbeing.
