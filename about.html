About This Project

Wellbeing Hub was created by a Grade 9 student who wanted to build something genuinely useful — a place where school students can find real help for stress, anxiety, and the everyday pressures of school life.

What is Wellbeing Hub?

Wellbeing Hub is a free, student-made website built to help school students aged 12–18 understand and manage stress, anxiety, and school-related pressure.

Unlike many wellbeing resources that feel formal or adult-focused, this site is written by a student, for students — using clear language, practical techniques, and honest advice that actually works in real school situations.

Everything here is evidence-informed and teen-appropriate. Whether you need a 2-minute breathing exercise before an exam, strategies to beat procrastination, or just want to understand why you feel anxious — this hub has something for you.

What Drives This Project

Mission

To give every school student access to practical, honest, and easy-to-understand wellbeing information — completely free, no sign-up, no fluff.

Vision

A school culture where students feel comfortable talking about stress, seeking help without stigma, and prioritising their mental health alongside their grades.

Values

Honesty, empathy, accessibility, and evidence. Nothing here is made up or exaggerated — every technique is grounded in what actually helps.

Core Principles

These six principles guided every decision in designing this site — from the content to the colours.

Student-First Language

Written like a conversation, not a textbook. No jargon, no lectures.

Evidence-Informed

Techniques like box breathing, grounding, and sleep hygiene are research-backed.

Accessible to Everyone

No app required, no subscription, no barriers. Just open the site and use it.

Calming Design

Soft colours, clean layout, and a light/dark toggle to suit any environment.

Non-Judgmental

This site never implies you are weak for feeling stressed. Stress is human.

Safety-Aware

Crisis resources and professional help links are prominent and easy to find.

Who Made This?

Hi — I'm a Grade 9 student (MYP Year 4) who built this website as a Service & Action project for my IB Middle Years Programme. I chose mental health and student wellbeing because I believe it's one of the most real and under-addressed challenges students face.

I built this entire site using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — designing the layout, writing all the content, and making sure everything feels supportive and easy to use. No templates, no builders — just code and a genuine desire to help.

If this site helped you in any way — even just one breathing exercise on a tough day — then it achieved exactly what it set out to do.

Why I Built This

The idea came from a simple observation: most students deal with stress and anxiety at school, but very few know what to actually do about it. You can't focus in class when your mind is racing. You can't perform well in exams when you're overwhelmed with panic.

Most mental health websites are written for adults, or they're so clinical that they feel distant. I wanted something that felt like a friend explaining things clearly — practical steps you can actually use, not just descriptions of what stress is.

The Service & Action component of the IB MYP pushed me to turn that idea into something real. This website is the result of weeks of research, writing, designing, and testing to make sure it genuinely helps.

"The most important thing I learned while building this was that helping others starts with actually listening to what they need — not just what you think they need."
— The Creator, Grade 9
